The [section] element is going to be split into multiple different ones and we will get rid of the play_mode field.  The [clip] element will remain the same.

Here are the rest of the elements and what their behavior should be:

[section] - this is equivalent of play_mode = learn

[break] - this is the equivalent to play_mode = solo and clip not empty.  To re-iterate this behavior: when the break is reached, all currently playing instruments stop and the wav file of the associated clip is played.  After loop_count iterations of that clip, we move on to the next block in the sequence.  During the break, the playfield will be empty, unless the next block is a [section] block and there are only 4 beats remaining, in which case the notes from the next section will begin to fall (the current behavior).

[reset] - this is the equivalent of play_mode = solo and clip = "".  All currently playing instruments stop and we immediately move on to the next block.

[background] - the specified clip should start playing when we get to the next break or section block - it should continue looping until there is a [break] or [reset] block (or the song is over).  We immediately move on to the next block.
